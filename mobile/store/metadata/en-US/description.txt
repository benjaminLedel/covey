covey is the app for people who work with AI agents on a covey server. On the server, each agent has a role in the organisation chart, a backlog and a workplace of its own. The app is where you talk to them.

The app needs a covey server: your organisation's own installation, or an organisation on app.covey.work. It does nothing on its own and has no sign-up. You pair it once from the web interface.

TEAM
- What waits for you comes first: questions and approvals from agents.
- Write to an agent the way you write to a colleague. It answers in the conversation, looks up a task or creates one, and tells you when the work is done. The full report stays one tap away.
- Conversations with colleagues, and groups in which people and agents write together. "@" addresses a member of a group.
- An agent that is waiting on you shows its question with an Answer button, and your reply goes to that question.
- Your colleagues by department, each with its state in words: working, sleeping, stopped.

NOTES
- Private notes, voice notes and meeting notes.
- Dictation is recognised on the iPhone or iPad, with a speech model the app fetches from your covey server. No audio is stored or sent.
- Where your server offers it, a meeting note is summarised into decisions and to-dos.
- A list and a board, with status, date and tags.

NOTIFICATIONS
- You are notified when an agent asks you something, answers you, or finishes or fails a task you gave it. The notification names the agent and what happened. The text of the message stays out of it unless your organisation switches on a preview.

PAIRING
- Scan the QR code the web interface shows under Profile & Settings, Mobile app. The code works once and for five minutes.
- The app receives an API key named after the device, with the rights of your seat. You can revoke it in the web interface like any other key.
- Without a camera at hand, the app also takes the server's address and an API key typed in by hand.
- The app connects to HTTPS addresses only.

PRIVACY
What you read and write stays on your covey server. The app contains no analytics and no advertising. Configuring agents, secrets and guard rails stays in the web interface; the app has none of it.

covey is open source under the AGPL-3.0. The server, this app and the documentation are on GitHub at github.com/benjaminLedel/covey.
