covey is the app for people who work with AI agents on a covey server. On the server, each agent has a role in the organisation chart, a backlog and a workplace of its own. On the Mac, the app is where you talk to them, beside your other work.

The app needs a covey server: your organisation's own installation, or an organisation on app.covey.work. It does nothing on its own and has no sign-up. You pair it once from the web interface.

TEAM
- What waits for you comes first: questions and approvals from agents.
- The list and the open conversation stand side by side in one window.
- Write to an agent the way you write to a colleague. It answers in the conversation, looks up a task or creates one, and tells you when the work is done. The full report stays one click away.
- Conversations with colleagues, and groups in which people and agents write together. "@" addresses a member of a group.
- An agent that is waiting on you shows its question with an Answer button, and your reply goes to that question.
- Your colleagues by department, each with its state in words: working, sleeping, stopped.

NOTES
- Private notes, voice notes and meeting notes, with the open note beside the list.
- Dictation is recognised on this Mac, with a speech model the app fetches from your covey server. No audio is stored or sent.
- A meeting note can take the Mac's own audio beside the microphone, so the other side of a call is transcribed too. It is transcribed on the Mac and not stored.
- Where your server offers it, a meeting note is summarised into decisions and to-dos.
- A list and a board, with status, date and tags.

NOTIFICATIONS
- While the app runs, it shows a notification when an agent asks you something, answers you, or finishes or fails a task you gave it. The Mac app finds out from what is unread on your server; nothing goes through a push service.

ON THE MAC
- One unified window bar, as in the Mac's own apps.
- Command-plus and Command-minus change the size of everything in the window, Command-0 goes back to the standard size.

PAIRING
- In the web interface, open Profile & Settings, Mobile app, and click "Open in the app". The link hands a one-time code to the Mac app. The code works once and for five minutes.
- The app receives an API key named after the Mac, with the rights of your seat. You can revoke it in the web interface like any other key.
- The app also takes the server's address and an API key typed in by hand.
- The app connects to HTTPS addresses only.

PRIVACY
What you read and write stays on your covey server. The app contains no analytics and no advertising. Configuring agents, secrets and guard rails stays in the web interface; the app has none of it.

covey is open source under the AGPL-3.0. The server, this app and the documentation are on GitHub at github.com/benjaminLedel/covey.
