# Code of conduct

This project follows the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
version 2.1. The short version:

**Be decent to each other.** Assume the person on the other side is competent
and acting in good faith. Criticise the code, the design or the argument — not
the person. Harassment, personal attacks and discrimination are not welcome
here, in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else the project meets.

Disagreement is fine and useful; this is a project with strong opinions written
down in `spec/`, and it gets better when people push back on them. Do it on the
merits.

If you experience or witness behaviour that breaks this, report it to
<benjamin@schule-plus.com>. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may
edit, hide or delete contributions that do not fit, and may block accounts from
the project when that is the proportionate answer.
